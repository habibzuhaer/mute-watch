Glossary — what the terms mean
Money weight / mcap
Token market cap in USD. Filter: micro <$350K … ≥$60M. «unknown» — not in scanner cache.
Score / tier
Rating 0–100: act (≥55), watch (medium), noise (weak). The «Why» column lists reasons.
Deployer / cluster
Who created the contract. Many tokens or pumps from one address = cluster, worth a closer look.
ATH
All-Time High — peak price. If ATH was long ago and far away — the token is already «pumped out».
MM / CEX / seed
Market maker/fund · exchange · a pre-known address from the watchlist.
Journal / hit-rate
Your enter/skip buttons. Hit-rate — how often the decision turned out right.
Expand / match
Expand — search for neighbours of known addresses. Match — a found link to a label.
