Glossary — what the terms mean
Money weight / mcap
Token market cap in USD. Filter above: micro <$350K → $60M+. «unknown» — token not yet in scanner cache.
Score / tier
Rating 0–100 and shelf: act (≥55), watch (≥35), noise (below). reasons explain the points.
MM / seed
Market maker / fund (DWF, Jump…). Seed — known watchlist address, not guessed.
CEX
Centralized exchange (Binance, Gate…). OUT to CEX = transfer to exchange (sometimes before sale/listing). IN from CEX = withdrawal.
Deployer
Address that deployed the token contract. One deployer for many tokens/pumps — suspicious cluster.
Expand / 1-hop
Graph expand: look at counterparties of known addresses one step out (neighbours).
2-hop / same-amount / fresh
Prep obfuscation: A→B→C with nearly the same amount (±0.5%), fresh intermediate EOA, or OUT to a bridge. After a hit the scanner queues neighbours as expand2.
Bridge
Cross-chain bridge (Stargate, Wormhole…). Bridge transfers often break the trail on one chain — score gets a bump.
Match
Found link of a token/wallet to a known label (MM, CEX, seed).
ATH
All-Time High — peak token price. ATH date / age help tell if it already pumped.
Prep
Pump-prep: hypothesis that price move prep is underway (fresh MM/CEX touches on a young token).
Hub / journal
Hub — summary page. Journal — your enter/skip marks to track hit-rate.
Cycle ok / stale
ok — processes alive and data fresh. stale — state not updated for long. stopped — scanner off.
Noise
Weak matches. Hidden from Live and not sent to Telegram.
Pass-2
Second scanner pass: finishes clusters and young tokens when the main queue is empty.
